# Sourdough in Order

This small handbook treats a first naturally leavened loaf as one continuous sequence rather than a collection of competing techniques. It begins with the starter, moves through mixing, fermentation, shaping, proofing, baking, and storage, and ends with a compact troubleshooting pass. The order matters because each decision changes the next one: a warm, active starter shortens bulk fermentation, a strong mix changes how the dough handles during shaping, and the final proof determines how the loaf opens in the oven.

The intended reader has a kitchen scale, a bowl, a bench scraper, and a heavy covered pot. No exotic flour blend or exact brand is required. Start with [a healthy starter](starter.md), keep notes on time and temperature, and treat the first few loaves as observations rather than verdicts on your ability.
