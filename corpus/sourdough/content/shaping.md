# Shape the Loaf

Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and use a bench scraper to coax it into a loose round. Let it rest uncovered or loosely covered for fifteen to twenty minutes so the outer gluten can relax. This pre-shape is not meant to create the final loaf; it simply makes the final shape more even. If the dough spreads immediately into a thin sheet, use less flour on the bench and build tension with smaller, firmer movements.

For a round loaf, fold the edges toward the center, flip the dough seam-side down, and drag it gently toward yourself in short motions so friction tightens the skin. Rotate and repeat until the surface feels taut but not torn. Flour the top and place the loaf seam-side up in a lined basket or a bowl lined with a floured towel. The seam position matters because the loaf will be inverted before baking, leaving the smooth side ready for scoring.
