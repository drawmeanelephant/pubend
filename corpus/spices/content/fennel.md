# Fennel

Fennel seed is the dried seed of the fennel plant, with a sweet anise aroma that varies from green and herbal to warm and almost candy-like depending on freshness and origin. It is common in Italian sausage, Indian spice mixtures, breads, pickles, and digestive seed blends. Unlike star anise, fennel can be used in generous pinches without overwhelming a dish, although its sweetness becomes obvious if combined carelessly with other sweet spices.

Fennel seeds benefit from light toasting, which reduces any raw herbal edge and brings out their nutty side. They pair naturally with [coriander](coriander.md) in vegetable and legume dishes because both spices sit in the bright, citrusy end of the spectrum. The connection to [star-anise](star-anise.md) is useful when substituting in a broth or braise, but fennel is not a one-for-one replacement because it is milder. With fenugreek, fennel helps soften bitterness and makes complex seed mixtures feel more rounded.
