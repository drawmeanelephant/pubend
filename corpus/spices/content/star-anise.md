# Star Anise

Star anise is the star-shaped fruit of an evergreen tree native to parts of China and Vietnam. Its flavor is intensely reminiscent of licorice because it is rich in anethole, but good star anise also has a warm, sweet, almost balsamic depth. The whole pods are attractive and useful because they can be added to broths, braises, syrups, and infusions and then removed. Broken points are normal, though very dusty or pale pods may be old.

Star anise is powerful, so restraint matters. In savory cooking it pairs with [cinnamon](cinnamon.md) and cloves, especially in broths and braised meats where a sweet aromatic edge balances salt and fat. Its resemblance to [fennel](fennel.md) is real but not total: fennel seed is greener, lighter, and less concentrated, while star anise is deeper and more forceful. Ground star anise disperses quickly, making small measuring errors much harder to correct.
