# Callitropsis

Callitropsis is the genus name used here for Alaska yellow-cedar, though the species has also been placed in Cupressus and Chamaecyparis. That shifting nomenclature reflects real uncertainty and changing evidence about relationships within Cupressaceae, so older field guides may use different scientific names for the same tree. Regardless of genus, the group is marked by scale-like leaves, pendant or irregular branchlets, and small roundish cones that mature slowly. The foliage is usually arranged in loose, flattened sprays rather than the very regular fans of western redcedar.

In this guide the genus is represented by [Alaska yellow-cedar](callitropsis/nootkatensis.md), a long-lived montane and northern coastal tree. Its identification depends on a combination of drooping foliage, cone shape, bark texture, habitat, and the yellowish color of its wood rather than any single character.
