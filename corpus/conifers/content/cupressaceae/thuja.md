# Thuja

Thuja is a genus of evergreen trees with flattened, fan-like sprays of scale leaves. The foliage is aromatic when crushed, and the small cones are narrow, woody, and made up of overlapping scales that open at maturity. Unlike needle-bearing members of Pinaceae, Thuja presents a soft, fern-like surface texture even on large branches. The genus name is associated with trees often called arborvitae or cedar, though common-name use varies by region and can be confusing when unrelated species are also called cedar.

The Pacific Northwest representative is [western redcedar](thuja/plicata.md), a species of major ecological and cultural significance. Its account emphasizes the spray geometry, cone form, bark character, and habitat clues that separate it from other scale-leaved trees in the region.
