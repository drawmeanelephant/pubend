# Selected Pacific Northwest Conifers

This guide is a compact, taxonomically arranged field companion to selected conifers of the Pacific Northwest. It is deliberately not exhaustive: the entries follow a small set of representative lineages from the wet coastal lowlands into colder montane and subalpine habitats. Because conifer identification often begins with family-level clues and only then narrows to genus, species, and variety, the guide is organized as a nested taxonomy rather than as an alphabetical list. Each level adds one diagnostic layer, so a reader can move downward through the hierarchy while comparing needles, cones, buds, bark, and habitat.

The three family accounts are [Pinaceae](pinaceae.md), the pine and fir family; [Cupressaceae](cupressaceae.md), the cedar and juniper family; and [Taxaceae](taxaceae.md), the yew family. The selections favor species a field naturalist is likely to encounter in Oregon, Washington, British Columbia, and adjacent interior mountain ranges, while keeping the taxonomy compact enough to use as a working reference.
