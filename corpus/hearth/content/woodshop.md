# Woodshop

The woodshop collection covers the small, sharp, well-ordered shop rather than a industrial cabinetry. Its center of gravity is tool choice, sharpening, sound joinery, and finishes that suit household objects. A modest bench, a few sharp edge tools, reliable measuring habits, and dry lumber accomplish more than a room full of machines used without a plan. Begin with [tool setup](woodshop/tool-setup.md), keep edges ready through [sharpening](woodshop/sharpening.md), and then practice structure through [joinery](woodshop/joinery.md).

The collection is written for repair and utility as much as for new projects. Chairs loosen, drawer boxes rack, handles split, and drying frames warp. Those problems are often small when caught early and large when ignored. A finish is not decoration alone; it controls moisture and makes cleaning possible, which is why [finishes](woodshop/finishes.md) belongs in the core sequence. The shop also serves other household crafts. A simple rack built with sound joints can support food drying, while smooth wooden tools turn up throughout the textile crafts.
